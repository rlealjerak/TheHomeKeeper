Privacy Policy for TheHomeKeeper

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

Introduction

TheHomeKeeper ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Create an account (email address, name, username)
  • Use the app to track maintenance items
  • Contact us for support

Specific Data Collected:

  • Account Information: Email address, first name, last name, username, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: User preferences, notification settings
  • Maintenance Data: Item names, categories, notes, maintenance dates, frequencies
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version

Automatically Collected Information

When you use TheHomeKeeper, we automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Features used, actions taken, time spent in app
  • Device Data: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs (via Firebase Crashlytics)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  1. Provide Services: Create and manage your account, store your maintenance data
  2. Send Notifications: Remind you about upcoming maintenance tasks
  3. Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, add features
  4. Communicate: Send important updates, respond to support requests
  5. Security: Prevent fraud, protect against unauthorized access

Data Storage and Security

Storage

  • Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Servers located in: United States

Security Measures

  • Password encryption using industry-standard algorithms
  • Secure authentication via Firebase Authentication
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • No sharing of credentials with third parties

Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained as long as account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Backups: Removed from backups within 90 days of account deletion

Your Rights and Choices

Account Management

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your personal data in the app
  • Update: Modify your profile information in Settings
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data (contact us)

Notification Control

You can:

  • Enable/disable maintenance reminders in Settings
  • Manage notification preferences in device settings
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

Data Deletion

To delete your account:

  1. Open TheHomeKeeper app
  2. Go to Settings → Edit Profile
  3. Scroll to bottom → Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion

What gets deleted:

  • All maintenance items you created
  • Your profile information
  • Your account credentials
  • All personal data from our servers

Third-Party Services

TheHomeKeeper uses the following third-party services:

Firebase (Google LLC)

  • Purpose: Authentication, database, analytics
  • Data Shared: Email, user ID, app usage data
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

React Native (Meta Platforms, Inc.)

  • Purpose: App framework
  • Data Shared: None directly

Notifee (Invertase)

  • Purpose: Local notifications (processed on your device)
  • Data Shared: None (notifications are local)

Children's Privacy

TheHomeKeeper is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using TheHomeKeeper, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence.

For EU/UK Users:

  • We comply with GDPR requirements
  • Data transfers use Standard Contractual Clauses
  • You have additional rights under GDPR (see "Your Rights" section)

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:

  • Right to Know: What personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of privacy choices

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email (for material changes)

Your continued use of TheHomeKeeper after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Data Breaches

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will:

  1. Notify affected users within 72 hours
  2. Describe the nature of the breach
  3. Provide remediation steps
  4. Report to relevant authorities as required by law

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:

Email: support@thehomekeeper.app

For GDPR/CCPA Requests: Include "Privacy Request" in the subject line and specify your account email, type of request (access, deletion, export), and verification information.

Summary

What we collect: Email, name, username (account creation); maintenance items you create; device and usage information.

Why we collect it: Provide maintenance tracking services, send you reminders, improve the app.

How we protect it: Encrypted storage (Firebase), secure authentication, no data selling, you can delete anytime.

Your control: View, update, or delete your data; control notifications; export your data on request.
